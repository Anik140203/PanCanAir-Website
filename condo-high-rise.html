Systems we manage

Every box. Every loop.

Centralized mechanical rooms in high-rise condos are intricate. Here's what's covered the day you sign.

Boilers + Hydronic
Cast iron, fire-tube, condensing. Glycol loops, expansion tanks, primary/secondary piping, header isolation.
Chillers
Air- and water-cooled, scroll/screw/centrifugal. Cooling tower management, condenser water loops, BAS-integrated.
MAUs
Makeup air units in penthouse stacks. Burner assemblies, filter banks, gas valves, dampers, full controls integration.
Pumps
Inline, base-mounted, VFD-driven. Seal replacement, alignment, balancing, vibration analysis.
Controls + BAS
DDC controllers, sequence-of-operation tuning, alarm management. We work with Distech, Siemens, Honeywell, Tridium.
RTUs
Rooftop package units for amenity floors, mixed-use commercial. Belt drives, economizers, refrigerant work, recovery.
Cooling Towers
Open and closed-loop, FRP and galvanized. Basin cleaning, fill replacement, water treatment, Legionella programs.
Pressure Vessels
TSSA-registered tanks, expansion tanks, hot water cylinders. Annual inspections, certifications, renewals.
